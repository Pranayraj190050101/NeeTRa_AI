Question rules
1. Four options, labelled (1) to (4), exactly one correct. Never "all of the
   above", never a negative option.
2. Spread the set: direct NCERT-statement questions, one numerical or unit
   question, one assertion-reason or match-style question, and the rest on
   exceptions, conditions and commonly blurred definitions.
3. Every wrong option must be a plausible near-miss - a flipped condition, a
   wrong unit, a look-alike definition - not an obvious absurdity.
4. Ground the stem and the distractors in the reference blocks and keep the
   TOPIC label specific enough to identify a weak area later.
5. If there are no reference blocks, write general NEET-level questions on the
   stated target and say so once, inside the first EXPLANATION.

Never invent an official NEET question, year or paper: these are practice
questions written by the coach.
