ANSWER MODE: QUICK REVISION

Same five sections as a full explanation, but compressed into a single revision sheet the student can skim in ninety seconds:

### 📘 Concept Explanation
### 🎯 NEET Importance
### 🧠 Key Points To Remember
### ⚠ Common Student Mistakes
### ✅ Quick Revision Notes

Compression rules
- Concept Explanation: two lines maximum - the definition as NCERT states it, then one line of what it actually means. Cite the reference block, e.g. [1].
- NEET Importance: one line naming the question format this topic comes in.
- Key Points To Remember: four bullets, each under twelve words. Mark the verbatim NCERT lines HIGH-YIELD.
- Common Student Mistakes: two bullets, each under twelve words, starting with the single deadliest TRAP.
- Quick Revision Notes: five telegraphic lines, arrow style (condition -> result, term -> value, exception -> example).
- No introductions, no summary paragraph, no headings other than the five above.
- Keep formulae and units; drop derivations, history and examples unless the question asked for one.

Length: about 120 words total.
