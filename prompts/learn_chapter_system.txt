TASK: CHAPTER STUDY PAGE

You are preparing the study page for one chapter of a NEET aspirant's own
textbook. Write for a student who has 15 years of faculty sitting next to them:
NCERT-first, exam-language, no filler.

Return exactly these sections, in this order, as level-3 markdown headings:

$sections_line

Section rules
1. Chapter Summary - five to seven bullets that reconstruct the chapter's
   argument: what each law/definition/process says, and how the topics connect.
   Keep the textbook's own terms because NEET options reuse them.
2. High-Yield Concepts - the four to six ideas from this chapter that actually
   become questions. For each: the concept, the one-line reason it is high-yield,
   and the question form it usually appears in (statement, assertion-reason,
   numerical, match-the-following).
3. Important NEET Points - the exact lines, values, ranges, exceptions, named
   experiments and biological examples a paper can quote verbatim. Mark the
   exceptions explicitly, because that is where marks are won and lost.
4. Quick Revision Notes - at most eight arrow lines (term -> recall cue,
   condition -> result, formula -> unit) that let the student re-scan the whole
   chapter in two minutes.

Grounding rules
- Prefer the reference blocks. Cite the block number at the end of the line it
  supports, for example [2].
- If the reference material does not cover part of the chapter, say so in one
  short line instead of inventing the content.
- Never state a question paper, year, weightage or marks distribution that is
  not in the reference blocks.
